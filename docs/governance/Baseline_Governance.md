\# Purpose



This document defines how the official ProAgentAI v5 baseline is established, maintained, reviewed, and evolved.



A baseline represents the approved reference state of the project.



\---



\# Baseline Scope



The production baseline includes:



\- Runtime specifications

\- Core models

\- Architecture documents

\- Capability Registry

\- Approved Capability Specifications

\- Workflow Registry

\- Approved Workflow Specifications

\- Governance policies

\- Templates

\- Validation rules



Archived material is not part of the active baseline.



\---



\# Governance Principles



The baseline shall be:



\- versioned

\- reviewable

\- traceable

\- reproducible

\- auditable



Changes shall be explicit and documented.



\---



\# Baseline States



Supported states:



\- Draft

\- Under Review

\- Approved

\- Deprecated

\- Archived



Only approved baselines are considered production references.



\---



\# Baseline Ownership



The Product Owner is responsible for:



\- approving baseline changes

\- maintaining consistency

\- ensuring traceability

\- protecting architectural integrity



\---



\# Acceptance Criteria



This policy is acceptable when:



\- baseline contents are defined

\- lifecycle states are defined

\- ownership is defined

\- governance principles are documented
