\# Purpose



Architecture Review evaluates the proposed solution architecture before implementation.



The objective is to verify that the architecture is complete, consistent, traceable, and aligned with project requirements.



Architecture Review does not redesign the solution.



It assesses its quality and readiness.



\---



\# Objective



The capability shall:



\- review architecture completeness

\- verify requirement traceability

\- identify architectural inconsistencies

\- identify missing components

\- review interface definitions

\- review architectural assumptions

\- review architectural risks

\- produce review findings

\- recommend approval or rework

The capability is successful when:



\- architecture has been reviewed

\- findings are documented

\- recommendations are actionable
