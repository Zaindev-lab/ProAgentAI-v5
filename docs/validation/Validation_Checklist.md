\# Purpose



This checklist defines the minimum validation activities before a project, document, capability, workflow, or release can progress.



\---



\# Documentation Validation



\- \[ ] Document ID exists

\- \[ ] Document ID is unique

\- \[ ] Version exists

\- \[ ] Status exists

\- \[ ] Owner exists

\- \[ ] Category exists

\- \[ ] Dependencies are valid

\- \[ ] References are valid

\- \[ ] Required sections exist



\---



\# Architecture Validation



\- \[ ] Architecture documents are complete

\- \[ ] Component responsibilities are defined

\- \[ ] Dependencies are documented

\- \[ ] Runtime interactions are defined

\- \[ ] Capability references are valid



\---



\# Capability Validation



\- \[ ] Capability is registered

\- \[ ] Inputs are defined

\- \[ ] Outputs are defined

\- \[ ] Preconditions exist

\- \[ ] Postconditions exist

\- \[ ] Acceptance criteria exist

\- \[ ] Validation requirements exist

\- \[ ] Limitations are documented



\---



\# Workflow Validation



\- \[ ] Workflow is registered

\- \[ ] Capability sequence is valid

\- \[ ] Approval gates exist

\- \[ ] Validation gates exist

\- \[ ] Completion conditions exist



\---



\# Governance Validation



\- \[ ] Required approvals exist

\- \[ ] Baseline is current

\- \[ ] Version is correct

\- \[ ] Change management requirements are satisfied



\---



\# Security Validation



\- \[ ] Security review completed

\- \[ ] High-risk findings documented

\- \[ ] Required mitigations identified



\---



\# Evidence Validation



\- \[ ] Evidence is available

\- \[ ] Evidence is traceable

\- \[ ] Evidence level is appropriate

\- \[ ] Unsupported claims are absent

\# Release Validation



\- \[ ] Validation scripts pass

\- \[ ] Known limitations documented

\- \[ ] Risks documented

\- \[ ] Release notes complete
